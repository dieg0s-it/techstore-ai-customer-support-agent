# TechStore – Returns and Refunds Policy

**Document:** Returns Policy  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Company:** TechStore (Fictional Company)

## 1. Return Period

TechStore offers a voluntary 30-calendar-day return period starting from the date the customer receives the product.

This commercial policy does not limit any mandatory consumer rights applicable under the customer's local legislation.

## 2. Return Conditions

Products can be returned when:

- They are in good condition.
- They include all original accessories.
- They include the original packaging, whenever reasonably possible.
- The customer provides proof of purchase.

Products do not need to be unused when the return concerns a defect or a lack of conformity.

## 3. Return Procedure

Customers should:

1. Contact TechStore Customer Support.
2. Provide their order number.
3. Explain the reason for the return.
4. Wait for return instructions.
5. Send the product following the instructions provided by the support team.

Customers should not send products to an address without receiving return authorization.

## 4. Refund Processing

Once the returned product has been received and inspected:

- TechStore will notify the customer about the return decision.
- Approved refunds will be processed using the original payment method.
- Refund processing normally takes 5–10 business days after approval.
- The time required for the payment provider to display the refund may vary.

## 5. Damaged or Defective Products

If a product arrives damaged or develops a defect:

- Customers should contact Customer Support.
- They should describe the problem.
- Photographs may be requested when appropriate.
- The case will be reviewed by the support team.

Defective products must be handled under the applicable consumer protection rules and warranty conditions.

## 6. Exceptions and Special Cases

The following cases require individual review:

- Products showing signs of misuse.
- Missing accessories.
- Products with physical damage unrelated to a manufacturing defect.
- Products with special customization.

No return request should be automatically rejected without reviewing the applicable consumer rights.

## 7. Customer Support Escalation

The AI assistant must escalate cases when:

- The customer disputes a return decision.
- A refund has not arrived within the expected period.
- The product is defective or damaged.
- The customer requests an exception.
- The applicable policy is unclear.

The AI assistant cannot approve refunds or authorize returns.

**Important:** This is a fictional training policy created for the TechStore portfolio project. It must be reviewed against applicable legislation before any real-world use.

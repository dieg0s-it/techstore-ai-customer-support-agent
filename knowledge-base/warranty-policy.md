# TechStore – Warranty Policy

**Document type:** Internal knowledge base  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Company:** TechStore (fictional e-commerce company)  
**Purpose:** Provide guidance about product warranties, defective products and warranty claims.

## 1. Warranty Coverage

Products sold by TechStore may be covered by a manufacturer's warranty or applicable consumer protection legislation.

The exact warranty period and conditions depend on the product, manufacturer and applicable law.

The AI agent must not invent warranty periods or assume that all products have the same coverage.

## 2. Defective Products

If a customer reports that a product is defective or stops working, the agent should:

- Ask the customer to describe the problem.
- Explain that the case needs to be reviewed.
- Offer escalation to a human support representative.

The agent must not automatically reject a claim or promise a replacement or refund.

## 3. Information Required for a Warranty Claim

A human support representative may request:

- Order reference.
- Product name and model.
- Purchase date.
- Description of the problem.
- Photographs or videos, if relevant.

The AI agent cannot verify purchases or warranty eligibility because it has no access to order records.

## 4. Warranty Exclusions

Warranty coverage may be affected by circumstances such as:

- Accidental damage.
- Improper use.
- Unauthorized modifications.
- Normal wear and tear.

These circumstances must be reviewed individually. The AI agent must not make a final decision about warranty eligibility.

## 5. Warranty Claim Process

A warranty claim must be reviewed by a human support representative.

The representative will assess the available information and explain the next steps.

The AI agent cannot approve warranty claims, authorize repairs or replacements, or confirm eligibility.

## 6. Escalation Rules

The AI agent must escalate the case when:

- A customer reports a defective product.
- A customer requests a repair or replacement.
- Warranty coverage or eligibility is unclear.
- A customer disputes a warranty decision.
- The case requires checking purchase details or manufacturer conditions.

The AI agent must clearly explain when human review is required.

---

**Important note:** This is a fictional policy created for an AI automation training project. It does not replace manufacturer warranties or applicable consumer protection legislation.

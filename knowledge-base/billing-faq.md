# TechStore – Billing FAQ

**Document type:** Internal knowledge base  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Company:** TechStore (fictional e-commerce company)  
**Purpose:** Provide guidance about invoices, payments, charges and billing issues.

## 1. Accepted Payment Methods

TechStore displays the available payment methods during checkout.

The AI agent must not assume that a particular payment method is available for every order or destination.

If a customer asks about a specific payment method, the agent should explain that availability must be confirmed during checkout or by a human support representative.

## 2. Payment Confirmation

Customers receive an order confirmation email after a successful purchase.

If a customer has been charged but has not received an order confirmation, the case should be reviewed by a human support representative.

The AI agent cannot access payment records or verify individual transactions.

## 3. Invoices and Receipts

Customers who need an invoice or receipt should contact customer support.

A human support representative can review the order and explain how the document can be obtained.

The AI agent cannot generate invoices or access customer billing records.

## 4. Incorrect or Duplicate Charges

If a customer reports an incorrect or duplicate charge, the agent should:

- Acknowledge the concern.
- Ask the customer to describe the issue.
- Avoid requesting or exposing complete payment card details.
- Offer escalation to a human support representative.

The agent must not confirm that a charge is incorrect or promise a refund without verification.

## 5. Failed Payments

If a payment fails, the customer should check the payment information and contact their payment provider if necessary.

If the issue persists, customer support can review the case.

The AI agent cannot verify the reason for a failed transaction.

## 6. Refunds and Billing Adjustments

Refunds must follow the applicable Returns and Refunds Policy.

The AI agent cannot approve refunds, modify charges or make billing adjustments.

A human support representative must review requests involving disputed charges or billing corrections.

## 7. Escalation Rules

The AI agent must escalate the case when:

- A customer reports an unexpected or duplicate charge.
- A payment appears to have been processed without an order confirmation.
- A customer requests an invoice that cannot be obtained through the normal process.
- A customer disputes a payment or refund.
- The case requires access to payment or billing records.

---

**Important note:** This is a fictional FAQ created for an AI automation training project. It does not represent a real company's payment or billing terms.

# TechStore – Shipping Policy

**Document type:** Internal knowledge base  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Company:** TechStore (fictional e-commerce company)  
**Purpose:** Provide accurate information about shipping, delivery times, tracking and delivery issues.

## 1. Shipping Methods and Delivery Times

TechStore offers the following fictional shipping options:

- **Standard Shipping:** Estimated delivery within 3–5 business days.
- **Express Shipping:** Estimated delivery within 1–2 business days.

Delivery estimates begin once the order has been processed and dispatched.

These are estimates, not guaranteed delivery dates.

## 2. Shipping Costs

Shipping costs depend on the delivery destination, shipping method and order details.

The exact shipping cost is displayed during checkout before the customer confirms the purchase.

The AI agent must not invent shipping prices or promise free shipping.

## 3. Order Processing

Orders are normally processed within 1–2 business days.

Processing may take longer during weekends, public holidays or periods of high demand.

A shipping confirmation email is sent once the order has been dispatched.

## 4. Order Tracking

Customers receive a tracking link by email when their order is dispatched.

The AI agent does not have direct access to TechStore's order management system or carrier tracking systems.

It must not claim to have checked an order's status or location.

If a customer needs an individual tracking update, the agent should explain this limitation and offer escalation to a human support representative.

## 5. Delayed Deliveries

If an order has not arrived within the estimated delivery window, the customer should contact customer support.

A human support representative must review the order and investigate the delay.

The AI agent must not assume that an order is lost or promise compensation.

## 6. Damaged or Missing Deliveries

If a package arrives damaged or appears to be missing items, the customer should contact support and describe the issue.

Photographs of the package or product may be requested by the support team.

The case must be reviewed by a human representative.

## 7. Delivery Address Changes

Customers should contact support as soon as possible if they need to change a delivery address.

Address changes cannot be guaranteed after an order has been processed or dispatched.

The AI agent must not promise that an address change has been completed.

## 8. Escalation Rules

The AI agent must escalate the case to a human support representative when:

- A delivery is significantly delayed.
- A package is reported as lost.
- An order arrives damaged or incomplete.
- A customer requests an address change after placing an order.
- The customer needs an individual order or tracking status check.
- The available information is insufficient to provide a reliable answer.

The AI agent cannot access order records, contact carriers or modify delivery information.

---

**Important note:** This is a fictional policy created for an AI automation training project. It does not represent a real company's shipping terms.
